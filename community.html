Community Guidelines & Rules

  • Be respectful to everyone: Treat all community members with kindness and professional empathy.
  • No spamming or advertising: Self-promotion, link drops, or unrelated marketing elements are strictly prohibited.
  • Ask questions related to learning only: Keep the channel topic focused on educational support, engineering design, or study assistance.
  • No offensive or harmful language: Keep conversation spaces safe, welcoming, and strictly inclusive.
  • Help others when possible: Foster community strength by answering queries and sharing logic tips with peers.
  • Admin decisions are final: Rules are actively managed by platform leads to sustain an optimal workspace environment.
